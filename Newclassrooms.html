8 schools · Kumi

Building safe classrooms for crowded schools

Through the foundation, we currently support children from eight different schools. Because we can sponsor such a large number of students at some schools, this gives the schools additional room to invest in improving their facilities, such as building extra classrooms.

In many schools, 100–200 students are taught by a single teacher, which makes learning extremely challenging. Thanks to your donations, we're building 3 classrooms in total across our partner schools — including a finished classroom at Adesso Primary School, and a new building underway at Omolokonyo. With each year of support, we're able to expand further.
