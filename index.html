Meet the founder

Aggie

Aggie has been a respected leader in the Ongino community in Kumi for many years, including through her work at Kumi Hospital supporting patients and students. Today she channels that same care into helping vulnerable children and families access education, working closely with other local leaders to reach those who need it most.
